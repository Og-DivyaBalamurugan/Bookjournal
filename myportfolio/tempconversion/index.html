Temperature Conversion








Conversion formula:


Fahrenheit to Celsius:

[°C] = ([°F] - 32) × 5/9


Celsius to Fahrenheit:

[°F] = [°C] × 9/5 + 32


Kelvin to Fahrenheit:

[°F]= 9/5(K - 273.15) + 32


Conversion formula:


Fahrenheit to Kelvin:

K = 5/9([°F] - 32) + 273.15


Kelvin to Celsius:

[°C]= K – 273.15)


Celsius to Kelvin:

K = [°C] + 273.15
